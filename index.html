About

"Great editing is invisible — it's felt, not seen."

I'm a video editor and motion designer with 5+ years of experience crafting content for brands, agencies, and creative studios. My work spans commercial brand films, social-first campaigns, documentary edits, and high-retention short-form content.

I specialize in narrative pacing, audience retention optimization, and motion graphics integration. Every project starts with the story — the software is just the tool to tell it well. I bring a cinematic visual sensibility to even the smallest deliverable.

Previously with Polygon Studios, Frame & Co Agency, and freelance work across the US and Europe.
